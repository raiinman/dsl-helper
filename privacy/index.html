Privacy policy

Effective October 20, 2026 · Dead Signal Interactive

This policy covers Dead Signal Interactive’s handling of information for DSL-Helper and its support requests.

It also explains the hosting of these public information pages. OpenAI and services you choose to connect handle information under their own policies. The separate deadsignal.studio website is outside the scope of this policy.

Routine plugin use

Dead Signal Interactive does not separately collect or store DSL-Helper chat content, usage analytics, or user data through the plugin outside OpenAI and the services users choose to connect. The distributed plugin contains guides, reference material, and a source-reader helper. It does not include a publisher-hosted MCP service.

If you contact support

We receive your email address, message, and any attachments you send. We use these to answer your request and troubleshoot DSL-Helper. Please do not send passwords, API keys, or unnecessary sensitive information.

Access is limited to people authorized to handle support and the email service used to deliver and store messages.

How long support messages are kept

We delete messages and attachments from the active support mailbox, including sent copies and trash, within 90 days after the issue is closed, unless the law requires longer retention. Information kept for a legal requirement is restricted to that purpose and deleted when the requirement ends. You can request earlier deletion by emailing michael@deadsignal.studio.

This timetable covers the active support mailbox. The email provider may keep residual backups under its own retention process. Deleting mailbox messages does not necessarily erase provider backups immediately.

OpenAI, GitHub, and connected services

Your host, such as ChatGPT or Codex, processes chats, selected files, and tool activity according to its applicable settings and policies. Guides may use tools and accounts already available in your conversation. Those providers can process information needed for the actions you request.

By default, DSL-Helper retrieves public guides from GitHub. The services carrying these requests receive ordinary connection and request information. Say “use the bundled guides only” to avoid retrieving guides online. This does not make ChatGPT or every connected service operate offline.

On a local host, the source-reader helper can write a source-version lock and explicitly requested output files. These are local files, not a publisher-hosted database. Manage them through your local environment.

These public information pages

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. We have not added analytics, tracking scripts, or forms. GitHub may process visitor network and request information, such as an IP address, to provide and protect its hosting service under its privacy statement. GitHub’s own retention practices apply to that information.

Questions and deletion requests

Email michael@deadsignal.studio about this policy or information you send to support. For information held by OpenAI or a connected provider, use that provider’s privacy controls and request process.
