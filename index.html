Dead Signal Library – Helper

More guides.
One helper.

DSL-Helper helps ChatGPT and Codex find practical guides for coding, design, 3D, games, AWS, media, marketing, and planning.

Made by Dead Signal Interactive, its bundled library contains 140 guides. The helper finds a guide that fits your request and reads its instructions so the assistant can use a more focused approach to your task.

How to use it

Enable DSL-Helper in your host and ask for help normally. You can also name a guide, such as “activate ponytail.” By default, guides are retrieved online and held at the same source version while you work on a task.

Say “use the bundled guides only” to avoid retrieving guides online. That setting controls guide retrieval; it does not make ChatGPT or other connected services operate offline.

Your tools, your control

A guide does not install an app, grant new permissions, or guarantee a correct result. Review generated work before relying on it.
