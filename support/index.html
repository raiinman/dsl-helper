Support

Effective October 20, 2026 · Dead Signal Interactive

Need help with DSL-Helper? Send a short description of the problem and we’ll use it to help troubleshoot.

Email support

michael@deadsignal.studio

What to include

  • The DSL-Helper version and whether you use ChatGPT or Codex.
  • What you asked it to do and what happened instead.
  • A short example and any error message.

Remove passwords, API keys, and sensitive personal or business information. A short example is usually better than an entire private chat. We do not promise a fixed response time.

How we handle your message

Dead Signal Interactive uses support messages to respond to your request and troubleshoot DSL-Helper. Access is limited to people authorized to handle support and the email service used to deliver and store messages.

We delete messages and attachments from the active support mailbox, including sent copies and trash, within 90 days after the issue is closed, unless the law requires longer retention. You can request earlier deletion at the same email address. The email provider may keep residual backups under its own retention process; deleting a mailbox message does not necessarily erase those backups immediately.
